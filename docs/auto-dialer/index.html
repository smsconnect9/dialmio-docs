Auto dialer

Call your whole lead list one by one, hands-free — from Assigned leads or the Leads tab.

Open the Auto Dialer

  1. Open Dialmio and tap Auto Dial in the bottom bar.
  2. You see all the leads assigned to you. At the top there is the Auto Dialer card — tap Start on it.
Have a list?Tap Have a list? Import Excel or CSV on the same screen to bring in numbers before you start.

How the Auto Dialer works

A short guide opens before your first session:

  • Calls your list one by one — tap Start once and every pending lead in your current filter is dialled for you, hands-free.
  • Waits while you log the call — after each call, set the status or add a remark. The next call starts only when you finish.
  • Marks leads complete for you — every dialled call completes the lead and updates it on your web CRM automatically.
  • Your pace, your SIM — set the gap between calls, the SIM to dial from, and speaker on or off in the dialler settings (gear icon).

Tap Start to continue.

Start calling

  1. The Auto-dialer screen shows how many leads are pending (for example 6 leads pending) and the Up next list.
  2. Untick any lead you want to skip. Use Filter, Due today, Sort or Product to choose which leads get dialled.
  3. Tap Start auto-dialer to call the whole list automatically, or Call next manually to dial one lead at a time yourself.

Auto dial from the Leads tab

  1. Go back and open Leads at the top.
  2. Tap Auto Dial in the top-right corner.
  3. Choose All, New or Contacted leads, then tap Start auto-dialer.
