Dashboard overview

A tour of the Dialmio web dashboard at app.dialmio.com — leads and reports, setup, import and Live Sheets.

The web dashboard is where owners and admins manage everything the team does in the Dialmio app. Open app.dialmio.com in your browser and sign in with your registered mobile number.

Leads: call logs, profiles & reports

  1. In the left menu, open Leads.
  2. Use the tabs to switch views: Call Logs, Lead Profiles, Reports, Employee Report, Call Logs Reports, Lead Profiles Reports, AI Calls, WhatsApp Contacts, Paid Clients, Followups, Team, Trash and Downloads.
  3. Filter by Today, Yesterday, 7 days, 30 days, This month, All time or your own dates, and by call type or employee. Tap Export to download.

The summary cards show Contacts, Total calls, Connected, Missed and Talk time, each compared with yesterday.

Setup

Open Setup in the left menu. Everything your team sees in the app is configured here:

TabWhat you manage
Message TemplatesWhatsApp messages your team can send, with category and attachment. Tap + New Template.
ProductsThe products or services you sell. Tap + New Product.
PackagesPlans with price and billing cycle. Tap + New Package.
Status TypesLead stages such as New, Contacted, Interested. Tap + New Status to add your own.
Lead Fields / Client FieldsExtra fields you want to store for each lead or client. Tap + New Field.
Permissions, Branch, EmployeesWho can see and do what, and which branch each employee belongs to.
WhatsApp Configs, Auto WA Rules, WhatsApp InboxConnect your WhatsApp Business API and set automatic messages.
Outbound APISend lead-status changes to your own CRM or software.
ApprovalOnly approved products, packages, statuses and fields are visible in the app. Pending ones stay in the dashboard until approved.

Import leads

  1. Open Import in the left menu.
  2. Give the import a name, then choose the source: Google Sheet (paste a sheet link shared as Anyone with the link), Upload file (.xlsx, .xls or .csv) or Paste from Excel.
  3. Map the columns, pick a product and the employees, and finish. Leads are shared among the selected employees round-robin.

Live Sheets

  1. Open Live Sheets in the left menu and tap + Connect a sheet.
  2. Connect a Google Sheet once. Every new row that comes into the sheet becomes a lead automatically and is assigned to your employees round-robin.
  3. Each connected sheet shows leads received, last sync and a Report. The Webhook log below shows every sync with rows new, updated, duplicate or invalid.
Other lead sourcesBolna Incoming and Custom Webhook in the same menu bring leads in from AI calls and other software.
